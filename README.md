## About

I'm a senior researcher and engineer building, evaluating, and shipping LLM systems at industrial scale. My work sits at the intersection of **LLM pipeline design**, **inference optimization**, and **quantitative evaluation of frontier models**, with a parallel practice in **Persian-first NLP** — because tokenizers that drop half an alphabet are bugs, not quirks.

As **Editor-in-Chief at Teknav**, I shape the Farsi-speaking world's most-read ML coverage and publish original research alongside it. Three-plus years of production ML, 28 long-form articles, 118k+ readers.

> **Operating principles** &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Evidence over enthusiasm.* &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Boring infrastructure, ambitious models.* &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Persian is a first-class citizen.* &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Editorial taste is technical taste.*
